# A Four-Week Plan for the Meta Interview

Four weeks at 10 to 12 hours per week is enough for an engineer who already solves medium coding problems. The plan spends two weeks on coding pace, one week on design, and one week on stories and full mock loops. Every week ends with a finish condition, which is a test you must pass before moving on.

Candidates who are new to coding patterns should add two to four weeks before week 1. Candidates for E3 can replace the design work in week 3 with more coding.

## The plan in one table

| Week | Focus | Hours | Finish condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coding patterns | 10 to 12 | You name the pattern for 8 of 10 unseen problems within two minutes |
| 2 | The two-problem pace | 10 to 12 | You solve two medium problems in 35 minutes, three sessions in a row |
| 3 | System design or product architecture | 10 to 12 | You complete two 45-minute designs aloud and cover every step |
| 4 | Stories and mock loops | 10 to 12 | You finish one full mock loop, and six stories each fit in two minutes |

## Week 1: coding patterns

- Study one pattern per day: sliding window, tree breadth-first search, tree depth-first search, merge intervals, two heaps, and K-way merge.
- Solve three problems per pattern from the [question bank](question-bank.md) without a timer.
- Start with the [introduction to the sliding window pattern](https://www.designgurus.io/course-play/grokking-the-coding-interview/doc/introduction-to-sliding-window-pattern?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=meta-interview-guide&utm_content=four-week-plan) and the [introduction to tree breadth-first search](https://www.designgurus.io/course-play/grokking-the-coding-interview/doc/introduction-to-tree-breadth-first-search-pattern?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=meta-interview-guide&utm_content=four-week-plan).
- Write one line per problem: the pattern, and the clue that reveals it.
- Ask the recruiter for the target level and the type of design round.

## Week 2: the two-problem pace

- Run five timed sessions of two problems in 35 minutes. Use a plain editor and speak aloud.
- Follow the time budget on the [coding interview](coding-interview.md) page: 2 minutes to clarify, 2 to plan, 9 to code, 3 to test.
- Take the problems from Grokking the Meta Coding Interview, such as [Diagonal Traverse](https://www.designgurus.io/course-play/grokking-meta-coding-interview/doc/diagonal-traverse-medium?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=meta-interview-guide&utm_content=four-week-plan) and [Minimum Window Substring](https://www.designgurus.io/course-play/grokking-meta-coding-interview/doc/minimum-window-substring-hard?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=meta-interview-guide&utm_content=four-week-plan).
- Record each failure as one of three causes: wrong pattern, slow coding, or a bug found too late.
- Repeat each failed problem three days later.

## Week 3: design

- Study the method first with the [system design master template](https://www.designgurus.io/course-play/grokking-the-system-design-interview/doc/system-design-master-template?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=meta-interview-guide&utm_content=four-week-plan).
- Work through three designs: [the news feed](https://www.designgurus.io/course-play/grokking-the-system-design-interview/doc/designing-facebooks-newsfeed?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=meta-interview-guide&utm_content=four-week-plan), [the messenger](https://www.designgurus.io/course-play/grokking-the-system-design-interview/doc/designing-facebook-messenger?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=meta-interview-guide&utm_content=four-week-plan), and [Instagram](https://www.designgurus.io/course-play/grokking-the-system-design-interview/doc/designing-instagram?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=meta-interview-guide&utm_content=four-week-plan). Attempt each one for 45 minutes before reading the solution.
- For a product architecture round, write the API and the data model for each design before drawing any component.
- For a system design round, add load estimates and one failure case to each design.
- Keep two timed coding sessions this week so that the pace is not lost.

## Week 4: stories and mock loops

- Write six to eight STAR stories. STAR means Situation, Task, Action, Result. See the [behavioral interview](behavioral-interview.md) page.
- Label each story with a Meta value and check its scope against the target level on the [levels](levels.md) page.
- Run one full mock loop in one day: two coding rounds, one design round, and one behavioral round.
- Review the [common mistakes](common-mistakes.md) page two days before the loop.
- Stop new material one day before the loop, and review only your pattern notes and story list.

## If you have less time

| Time available | What to keep |
|---|---|
| Two weeks | Week 2 and week 3, with stories written on two evenings |
| One week | Five timed coding sessions, one design, and four stories |
| Three days | One timed session per day, and the news feed design once |
