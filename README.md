Three things make the Meta loop different from other large-company loops. The coding rounds are fast: candidates commonly report two problems in about 35 minutes of a 45-minute round. The design round comes in two types, system design and product architecture, and the candidate should confirm the type with the recruiter. The level is decided by the loop, and the design and behavioral rounds carry most of the level decision from E5 upward.

This repository is a short, free guide to the whole loop. Each page takes about three minutes to read and ends with links to full lessons, articles, and answers. The guide is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or reviewed by Meta, and every process detail is commonly reported by candidates, not an official statement.

## The loop at a glance

| Stage | Length | What it measures | Guide page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recruiter conversation | 20 to 30 minutes | Background, motivation, and target level; no coding | [Interview process](interview-process.md) |
| Technical screen | 45 minutes | One or two coding questions in a shared editor | [Coding interview](coding-interview.md) |
| Full loop: coding | 2 rounds of 45 minutes | Speed, correctness, and clear reasoning on two problems per round | [Coding interview](coding-interview.md) |
| Full loop: design | 1 or 2 rounds of 45 minutes | System design or product architecture, from E4 or E5 upward | [System design interview](system-design-interview.md) |
| Full loop: behavioral | 1 round of 45 minutes | Fit with Meta values, conflict, ownership, and growth | [Behavioral interview](behavioral-interview.md) |
| Review and team match | Two weeks or more | The hire decision, the level, and the team | [Levels](levels.md) |

## FAQ

### How many rounds are in a Meta interview?

A software engineer candidate commonly reports six or seven conversations in total. These are one recruiter conversation, one technical screen, and a full loop of four or five interviews. The full loop usually contains two coding rounds, one or two design rounds, and one behavioral round. See the [interview process](interview-process.md).

### How hard is the Meta interview?

The difficulty comes from pace more than from unusual questions. Most coding questions are reported to be medium difficulty, and two of them must fit in one 45-minute round. Senior candidates are reported to fail on design more often than on coding. See the [coding interview](coding-interview.md) and [common mistakes](common-mistakes.md).

### How long does the Meta hiring process take?

Meta is reported to describe a typical process of about two to three months from first contact to decision. Each step usually adds one to two weeks, and team matching can add two weeks or more. See the [interview process](interview-process.md).

### What coding questions does Meta ask?

Meta is reported to ask well-known algorithm questions on arrays, strings, matrices, trees, and graphs. Common patterns include sliding window, tree breadth-first search, merge intervals, two heaps, and K-way merge. The [question bank](question-bank.md) lists 31 coding problems with links to worked solutions.

### Does Meta ask system design?

Yes, from about E5 upward, and the round has two types. System design covers distributed systems and scale. Product architecture, which Meta calls product design, covers a user-facing feature with its API and data model. See the [system design interview](system-design-interview.md).

### What level will Meta offer me?

Meta interviews each candidate against a target level, and the loop can confirm or lower it. Strong coding with weak design is the commonly reported cause of an offer one level lower. See [levels](levels.md).
